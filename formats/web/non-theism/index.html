Indagorism is non-theistic. It proposes no formal God, and the universe is our guidance.

On God

Indagorism neither affirms nor denies a deity. It names none, worships none, and asks no member to do either.

No member is required or encouraged to give up beliefs held outside Indagorism. What a member believes beyond this document is their own.

On origins and destiny

We do not claim to know where we come from or where we are going beyond what scientific evidence shows. Whatever lies past that evidence is a Lacuna: named, kept open, and discussed without conjecture.

On spirit

Indagorism looks for wonder in the universe itself. Attention to what is real is its spiritual practice.

On the meaning of life

Indagorism does not hand down a single meaning of life. Guidance in life comes from reason and from each member’s own beliefs and ideals.
