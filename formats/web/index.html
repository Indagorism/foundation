Indagorism

Indagorism (noun): from the Latin indagare, to track down and investigate, and the Greek agora, the gathering place.

Meaning is found in what is real, and measurement is how we come to know it.

Founding Document Version 1.0.0 20 November 2026

Contents

  1. 01 Preamble Indagorism holds that meaning is found in what is real. The natural world, the things around us, and our measurable relationship to both are sufficient ground for existence, happiness, and purpose.
  2. 02 The Creed Reality is knowable, and measurement is how we come to know it.
  3. 03 Non-Theism Indagorism is non-theistic. It proposes no formal God, and the universe is our guidance.
  4. 04 The Tenets Seven tenets turn the creed into daily practice.
  5. 05 Ethics Indagorism chooses three moral commitments: wellbeing, honesty, and stewardship.
  6. 06 Acceptance, Freedom, and Individual Rights Indagorism is founded on freedom of thought and on every person’s right to be fully human, free from the interference of others.
  7. 07 The Calendar The Indagorist year is marked by six astronomical observances and three days of measure.
  8. 08 Rituals and Practices Indagorist rituals mark life’s passages with facts that are both true and moving.
  9. 09 The Collective Indagorism is a collective. It belongs to its members together, and no person, office, or group owns it, speaks for it on their own authority, or decides for it alone.
  10. 10 Structure Indagorism has no prophets and no clergy. Authority rests in evidence and in the review of peers. Every role below exists to facilitate, as set out in The Collective.
  11. 11 The Living Canon This document is expected to change. Its current version is shown on its title page.
  12. 12 The Emblem The emblem of Indagorism is an error bar set inside a ring of ten marks.
