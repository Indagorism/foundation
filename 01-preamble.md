# Preamble

Indagorism holds that meaning is found in what is real. The natural world, the things around us, and our measurable relationship to both are sufficient ground for existence, happiness, and purpose.

Many traditions rest their meaning on mythology and conjecture. Indagorism rests it on what can be observed, measured, and shared. We do not claim certainty. We claim a method: look closely, measure honestly, and change our minds when the evidence does.

This document founds Indagorism, states what its members hold in common, and describes how it may be revised.
