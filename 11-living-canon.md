# The Living Canon

This document is expected to change. Its current version is shown on its title page.

1. Any Indagorist may propose an amendment in writing, with the reasoning and evidence for it.
2. The proposal is open to all members for comment and discussion. Reviews are convened as needed and take as long as they need.
3. The Council of Review facilitates the review. It gathers every view, helps members find common ground, and records any disagreement. It does not decide on the members' behalf.
4. A proposal is adopted, revised, or set aside when members reach common ground on it. The Council publishes the outcome and the reasoning behind it.
5. Adopted changes produce a new numbered version. Every prior version is kept on record.

The creed may be amended like any other part. A canon that cannot be corrected would contradict it.
